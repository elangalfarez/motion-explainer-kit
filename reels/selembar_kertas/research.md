## Conflicts and gaps

- **PTUN first-instance date:** ANTARA reports both 21 May 2026 (30 Oct article) and 12 May 2026 (11 Nov article). Use "Mei 2026" on screen unless the PTUN decision text is checked.
- **MA decision date in the ministry's letter:** detik quotes the revocation letter citing the cassation as dated "3 Oktober 2026". Every report on the ruling itself says 30 Oct 2026. Search results also mention Kompas quoting the letter as citing "724 K/TUN/2026". I could not open that Kompas page, so that number is unverified. 742 K/TUN/2026 and 30 Oct 2026 are the figures backed by the ruling.
- **"Published 10 Nov":** the 10 Nov date is the minutasi date, when the full ruling was sent to the originating court. Neither MA's directory (403) nor any report gives an upload timestamp. On screen, "putusan lengkap dirilis 10 November" matches the reports.
- **Who was sued:** The defendant is named differently: "Kementerian Pendidikan Dasar dan Menengah" (ANTARA 30 Oct) versus "Direktorat Jenderal Pendidikan Dasar dan Menengah, Kementerian Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan" (ANTARA 11 Nov). The 2019 issuer was Kemendikbud; Kemendikdasmen is its successor for this function.
- **Cassation filing date:** afu.id says 24 Aug 2026; Katadata (Antara copy) says "Permohonan didaftarkan pada 8 Oktober 2026". Not needed for the reel.
- **MA reasoning vs ministry account:** MA found no evidence of an ijazah/sertifikat from abroad. Katadata reports the ministry said it received an O-level certificate from Orchid Park. Say "MA: tak ada bukti ijazah luar negeri"; do not claim no document existed.
- **Letter R-61/Pres/11/2026:** Only ANTARA Ambon prints it. The MPR page quotes only the MPR letter it answers ("R-161K/PP.02/MPR/11/2026"), dated 23 Nov 2026 by MPR and CNN but "2025" in ANTARA Ambon's transcription.
- **Bloomberg Technoz** dates the 11 Nov HMP handover "Selasa"; 11 Nov 2026 is a Wednesday.
- **Separate PTUN case 255/G/2026/PTUN.JKT** (plaintiff PT Mercy Toga Kirana) was declared "gugur" on 12 Nov after the revocation. Do not confuse it with Subhan Palal's case.
- **MK ruling of early Oct 2026** on Gibran's diploma is mentioned in several reports (Katadata cites the MK finding of no convincing foreign diploma; NU Online's headline says the petition was "tidak dapat diterima"). This was not in the brief and I did not verify it. Keep it out of the reel or check it separately.
- **Hatta hook:** No primary source (1956 DPR records, Setneg) was opened. Three secondary sources agree on 1 Dec 1956 and on Hatta being the only VP to resign. "Wapres pertama yang mundur sejak Hatta (1956)" is supported. GNFI dates Hatta's first letter 20 Jul 1956; English Wikipedia (not opened, search snippet only) says 26 Jul.
- **Election date:** The 14 Feb 2024 polling date was not re-checked on a page this session.
