# Judge checklist (score each shot 0–10 per criterion; pass = every shot avg >= 8, no criterion < 6)
1 shape/silhouette — katana rig, shadow, arch, flap read instantly at phone size
2 staging — one focal point per beat; hero >= 40% of frame on the hit
3 timing — slow-slow-FAST-hold; hits frame-exact to beats.md
4 colour — candle world warm/neutral; one hot accent (molten) after the cut
5 motion direction — smear follows tip; ignition mouth→end; flap falls away; embers outward
6 texture — paper grain, dry-brush ink, halftone; nothing flat-vector-only
7 punctuation — >= 2 of shake / inversion / flash / flare on each hit
8 concept clarity — "shadow acts first, sword arrives late" reads with no text
9 would a motion designer post this?
Extra gates: no text in frame; no hard-cut double exposure; sound soft and aligned (measured, unheard).
