The 1.03-second audio-event gap between lock and split is intentional. The visual world keeps a visible thread tell and small push during that anticipation. Shutter samples are clamped at camera cuts to avoid a one-frame double exposure.

Ending copy: “The cut arrives late.” Four words, at least 2.2 seconds fully readable. The title stays fixed after settling; motion moves to paper fragments and the world behind it.
