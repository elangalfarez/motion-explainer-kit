# Lessons for the next motion study

- A delayed consequence is a relationship, not an excuse for an unchanged image. Keep a visible physical tell, such as a thread trembling, and give the camera a small designed approach.
- Make a convergence readable at output size. Thin, low-opacity short paths can be present in source yet absent to the viewer; calibrate against the approved dense gather system.
- Keep one cut equation shared by the reveal streak, foreshadowing and every world mask. Verify apparent angle in encoded frames, but do not change correct geometry just to match an approximate visual estimate.
- A flat colored gap can read as an overlay. Separate the ground, distant scenery, architecture and foreground, then let the opening expose a darker interior and parallax.
- A mechanical rig needs the collar at zero extension too. Do not condition the habaki drawing on a nonzero exposed-blade length.
- Recheck the closing composition after changing title scale or foreground offsets. A more legible title can create a new collision with objects that moved into its landing area.
- Keep intentional stillness and deliberate near-silence documented, while making anticipation visible. Technical compliance does not settle the aesthetic verdict.
- Analyze decoded final AAC around the actual cue times. A loudness report alone cannot verify event alignment.
- Explicitly request canvas-only fonts with `document.fonts.load(...)` before rendering, then await that readiness promise. `document.fonts.ready` alone may resolve before the canvas first requests a typeface, causing a fallback-to-real-font jump on the first title frame and seek-order differences.
- Clamp a frame's shutter samples to its current camera shot. Sampling both sides of a hard editorial cut produces a one-frame double exposure, even with an otherwise deterministic renderer.
- Resolve title collisions through composition first: clear objects from the landing region and move the baseline before introducing a large scrim. A haze can hide the subject but leave conspicuous ghost silhouettes.
