# The Cut Arrives Late

Original, self-initiated motion study. Not a client campaign or an anime recreation.

## Delivery
12.000 seconds, 1920×1080, 60fps, H.264 SDR. Pure-code Canvas2D, four-subframe motion blur. Original procedural foley, no voice or music. Editable offline preview and source, plus an illustrated case-study document.

## Creative direction
The katana moves almost imperceptibly; the consequence arrives after the blade has returned. The environment must remain intact until the delayed payoff. Hero weapon rather than a character. Carry the approved Scarlet Sheath's paper, fragmented ink, lacquer, crimson and gold material language into an original setting. This is an unbranded art study, not an Algan Topup advertisement.

Exact title: “The cut arrives late.” Title lands by 9.65 and stays until 12.0, at least 2.2 seconds readable. No other film copy, logo, production labels, or CTA.

| Time | Picture | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Lacquered scabbard, textured paper landscape, hanging red thread. Fine strokes converge at the guard. | A precise object in a layered world; tension without destruction. |
| 3–4.35 | Macro cut to guard; blade moves just enough to reveal steel. One thin streak, then restraint. | The action is tiny. |
| 4.35–6.08 | Steel returns on the same axis, guard locks. | The action appears finished. |
| 6.08–6.98 | Return wide; thread trembles; environment still intact. | Deliberate anticipation, not a new event. |
| 6.98–9.4 | Background, architecture and thread separate along the same diagonal, at different depth speeds. Crimson void appears. | The consequence is enormous and late. |
| 9.4–12 | World settles around the opened scar; title resolves and holds. | One quiet ending. |

## Sound
Soft wind/paper tension; restrained metallic scrape; click at 5.95; near silence before split; warm low impact at 6.98; descending paper fragments. Sound will be measured, not auditioned. No harsh broadband bursts or repeated full-screen flashes.

## Review
Judge the visible delayed relationship, shared cut direction, mechanical axis, continuity, material detail, title legibility and audio event alignment. A failed aesthetic gate remains a failed gate. Numerical render checks are not a visual quality score.
