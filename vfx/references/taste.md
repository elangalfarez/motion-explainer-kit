# Taste — the house definition of "cool" (use when no reference is given)

## Look: two worlds that cut into each other
| World | Palette | Texture |
|---|---|---|
| Ink-on-paper | paper #ecebe6, ink #141014, crimson #c8102e, gold #d9a441 | faint 14px dot grid, dry-brush strokes, fragmented print ink (not round blobs) |
| Dark molten | bg #0b090c, glow white-hot core → #ff8a3a → #ff3a10 | orange halftone dots, additive bloom, anamorphic streak |
Swap the accent for the brief (ice: #e8f6ff→#59c8ff→#1a4dff; poison: #f4ffd0→#9cff3a→#2a8a10) but keep the structure: neutral world + one hot accent + gold detail.

## Composition
Strong single focal point (guard, eye, logo). Hero fills 40–70% of frame on the hit. Diagonals over horizontals. Negative space before a hit.

## Rhythm (the thing generic agents get wrong)
Slow–slow–FAST–hold. Build 0.8–2 s, release in 3–8 frames, hold 10–30 frames. Never constant motion. 1 major hit per 2–4 s.
Ease: build eIn3, landings eOut5, snaps eIn2 over 2–3 frames. Particles ACCELERATE on arrival.

## Punctuation kit (every hit uses ≥2)
camera shake (decaying noise, 6–18 px) · 1–2 frame colour inversion · white flash frame with black slit · spike-burst wipe · halftone dissolve · stamp/seal slam with splatter · title card (serif kanji + brush latin, glitch in).
